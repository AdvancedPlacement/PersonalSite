basketGame

An NBA take on NFL Head Coach 09, built in Godot.

A head coach career game across a 30 team league. You pick an offensive and defensive philosophy, install called plays like Floppy, Iverson Cut and Pick and Roll, and your players slowly learn them. Games are simmed possession by possession using player ratings, openness and play execution, then replayed on an animated court with drives, dribble moves, dunks, fast breaks and blocks. There's a full season with standings, league leaders, box scores and a best-of-7 playoff bracket.

Grand Strat 2

Varenthal: a fantasy grand strategy game in Godot, from the Age of Ley to the Age of Steam.

Brings together two earlier projects, my first grand strategy prototype and my procedural world generator, into one game. Twelve nations, from the Gearwright Dominion of Skrank to the Holy See of Saint Ilvane, split a world of provinces and states with political, state and terrain map modes. Each state has its own population of races and professions, buildings that hire workers and take time to construct, and monthly income that adds up to a country's GDP. Pick a nation, run the clock, and save or load your game.

Luxium

Randomly Generated Map, Roguelike Upgrade Mechanics.

This was a cooperative project with my friends over one summer during my Junior year of college. It was a really fun group project and we all learned a lot about unity and game development. It was a bullet hell of sorts, with the player running through waves of enemies, like butterflies and fireballs, each with their own patterns and attacks. There was also a random upgrade system, the player getting stronger and stronger and earning a high score at the end of their run.

Mind Kingdom

A personal to-do list application built using Flutter/Dart.

This was my junior project, where me and my team developed a cross-platform to-do list application. It worked on most any resolution and had a number of really helpful features! Amongst those not listed in the images, there was a 'raid' system, where if the user missed a few tasks they wanted to complete, their village would end up burned down, reducing their population and showing visually the consequences of their actions. There was also a streak function, task difficulty & stats page that would break down a few stats for the user.
